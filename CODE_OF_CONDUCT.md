# Code of Conduct

We want SpriteShelf to be a welcoming and respectful community for everyone.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior.
- Communicate openly and constructively.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it by contacting the project maintainers privately at:

arthurrabelopro@gmail.com

Reports will be handled confidentially and with respect.

Thank you for helping us maintain a positive community environment.
